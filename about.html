01 Bio

Research in a few lines

I am a Ph.D. researcher at the Centre of Excellence in Disaster Mitigation and Management (CoEDMM), Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. My thesis develops an early warning system for forest fire events over the Indian Himalayan Region.

The work combines numerical weather prediction models with observational and reanalysis datasets. I also work on tropical cyclones and on disasters induced by climate change.

I was born and raised in Kollam district, Kerala.

I conduct workshops and give lectures on the WRF model, Python, R and Origin Pro at universities and colleges across India.
