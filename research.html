02 M.Sc. thesis

Cyclonic storms over the Arabian Sea

Master thesis at IISER Kolkata.

M.Sc. thesis

Investigation on the increased frequency of cyclonic storms over Arabian Sea (1982-2019)

Objectives

  1. Vorticity and divergence analysis during cyclonic days.
  2. Study of sea surface temperature, wind shear and accumulated cyclone energy.
  3. Synoptic analysis of the genesis potential parameter along the cyclone track.
  4. Future projection of cyclonic storms over the Arabian Sea and prediction of their severity.
  5. Analysis of path speed, CI number and central pressure of cyclones, with their future projection.

Dr. M.G. Manoj, Scientist-D, ACARR, CUSAT, Kalamassery, Cochin, Kerala, India
Dr. Sayantan Sarkar, Associate Professor, School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, IIT Mandi (at IISER Kolkata during the thesis)
